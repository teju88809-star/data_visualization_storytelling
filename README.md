# data_visualization_storytelling
Analyzed the Sample Superstore dataset to visualize sales, profit, regional performance, product categories, and customer segments. Identified key business insights and provided data-driven recommendations to improve profitability and support better business decisions.
Data Visualization and Storytelling

Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product categories, and customer segments.

Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Compare sales across product categories and regions.
- Identify low-profit and loss-making sub-categories.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

Dataset

The dataset contains information about shipping mode, customer segment, region, product category, sub-category, sales, quantity, discount, and profit.

Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Data Visualization
- Business Analytics

Key Analysis

- Category-wise sales and profit comparison
- Region-wise sales performance
- Sub-category profitability analysis
- Discount and profit analysis
- Customer segment comparison

Key Learnings

- Transforming raw data into meaningful business insights.
- Identifying profitability risks.
- Communicating findings through charts and visualizations.
- Making data-driven business recommendations.

Deliverable

"SampleSuperstore_Report.pdf" — A report containing visualizations, business insights, and recommendations.

Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how data visualization and storytelling can help businesses understand performance, identify opportunities for improvement, and make informed decisions.
